How it works: ebb current flows toward the inlet (out to sea), flood flows away from it. Each window is centered on the slack where the current flips, so you ride the tail of one current out and the start of the next one back, while currents are at their weakest.

These are astronomical predictions. Wind, rain runoff and storm surge shift them. Stay out of the St. Augustine and Matanzas inlet throats themselves, where the current runs hardest. Check the marine forecast, wear a PFD, and tell someone your float plan.
